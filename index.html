    What we're building

    Two products, one operating principle

    Recruiters lose whole days to resumes that never had a chance. The platform reads every application against what the role actually needs, then hands back a ranked shortlist with the reasoning attached. Volume stops deciding who gets looked at.

    Storefront tools priced for Western software budgets don't work for a seller in Lahore moving forty orders a week. This one drops the monthly fee and keeps what matters. Products, payments, orders.

    Some ideas need pressure before they need code. They get published here first, open for anyone to pick apart or help build. What survives the feedback gets built.

    Who We Are

    A product studio, not a software house.

    Most dev shops wait for a client brief, build it, hand it off, repeat. That's fine work but it's not ours. Every product in our pipeline exists because we found the problem first and decided to own the solution.

    The HR platform started because our own hiring process was a mess. The e-commerce builder came from watching local sellers pay absurd monthly fees for tools built for Western markets. Each product traces back to a specific frustration, not a pitch deck.

    Avennex is a small team that ships its own software. Revenue comes from products people pay for, not from billing hours to someone else's roadmap.

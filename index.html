What we build.

Most projects mix two or three of these. The stack depends on the job, and we'll tell you why we picked it.

Custom software

If the tool you need doesn't exist yet, we'll write it. Usually that's an internal system, a customer portal or a SaaS product.

AI integration

Models inside your product or back office, doing work your team used to do by hand. If one doesn't save real hours, it doesn't ship.

Workflow automation

Someone copies data between two systems every morning. Someone else rebuilds the same report each Friday. That's the work we hand to software.

Web and mobile apps

Websites, dashboards and apps for iOS and Android. We stay on after launch for the releases that follow.

Cloud and data

Databases, APIs and hosting that keep up when usage climbs. Moving you off an old setup is part of the job too.

Consulting and analytics

Still working out what to build? We'll study your process and your data first. Sometimes the honest answer is a smaller project.

Your team shouldn't have to change for the software.

Most automation projects stall because they ask people to work differently on day one. Our order is the reverse. First we sit with your team and map the process as it runs, including the steps nobody ever wrote down. Then the repetitive part goes to software.

AI only goes where it can carry real work. Each model gets measured against the task it took over, and calls that need judgement stay with your people.

Get the process right and the automation is the easy part.

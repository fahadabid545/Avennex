Automation that fits how you already work.

Most automation fails because it asks your team to change first. We go the other way. We map the process as it really runs, including the steps nobody wrote down, then hand the repetitive part to software.

AI goes where it does a real job. Every model we ship is measured against the work it replaces, and the people who used to do that work keep the decisions that need judgement.

Process first. Automation second. That order matters more than the model.

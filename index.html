Software, AI and automation

Software built around how you work. Not the other way round.

Custom software, AI tools and automation for startups and established businesses. We map how your team works, lay out the ways to fix what's slow, from a Zapier flow to a full build, and agree one plan with you.

What we build.

Projects often mix several of these. The tools depend on the job, and you'll hear why we picked them.

Custom software

If the tool you need doesn't exist yet, we'll write it. Usually that's an internal system, a customer portal or a SaaS product.

AI integration

Models inside your product or back office, doing work your team used to do by hand. Each one gets tested on your own data before anyone relies on it.

Workflow automation

Someone copies data between two systems every morning. Someone else rebuilds the same report each Friday. We automate that work in Zapier, Make or code, whichever fits.

Web and mobile apps

Websites, dashboards and apps for iOS and Android. We stay on after launch for the releases that follow.

Cloud and data

Databases, APIs and hosting that keep up when usage climbs. Moving you off an old setup is part of the job too.

Consulting and analytics

Still working out what to build? We'll study your process and your data first. Sometimes the right answer is a smaller project.

Map the process first. Automate second.

Automation stalls when it asks people to work differently on day one. So we start by sitting with your team and mapping the process as it runs, including the steps nobody ever wrote down. Then the repetitive part goes to software.

AI only goes where it can carry real work. Each model gets measured against the task it took over, and calls that need judgement stay with your people.

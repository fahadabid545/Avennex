About Avennex, briefly.

What does Avennex do?

Avennex is a software studio working since 2022 and based in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. It builds custom software, AI integrations and workflow automation for startups and established businesses, and runs products of its own.

How is a project priced?

After scoping. The problem and possible solutions come first, then your budget and timeline, then one agreed plan and price.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, whenever a client asks for one.

Who owns the code after the project?

That's set in the agreement with each client, before work starts.

Who should build your software?

Avennex, a digital agency, an offshore team, a freelancer or an in house hire, compared on what buyers ask about in a first call.

The difference, in short.

Freelancers suit small, clear jobs. An in house team makes sense when software is your product for years. Agencies fit large programs where software sits next to brand and marketing, and offshore teams compete on hourly rate when the spec is fixed. Avennex starts from the problem, lays out more than one way to solve it and prices the plan around your budget. Its own products run in public, so the standard of work is visible before anyone signs.

Matching the option to the job.

When someone else fits

  • An agency: software is one piece of a bigger push that also needs brand, media and content work.
  • An offshore team: the spec is detailed and fixed, someone on your side reviews the code, and hourly rate is the main concern.
  • A freelancer: the task is small, the spec is clear and you can review the work yourself.
  • An in house team: the software is what you sell and needs daily attention for years.

When Avennex fits

  • You need more than one skill, like design, AI and integrations, in one plan.
  • Hiring a team would take longer than the project.
  • You want one agreed price and one point of contact.
  • The software needs care after launch, without a full time hire.

Mixing them.

These aren't exclusive. Avennex can build the first version while you hire, then hand it to your in house team with documentation, on terms agreed at the start.

Some companies keep their own developers for daily changes and bring Avennex in for the bigger pieces.

Rate vs total cost.

Compare the total for the whole project, not the hourly rate. A lower rate with more hours and rework, or a salary for a role you only need for six months, often costs more in the end.

Count the time you'd spend managing the work too. It never shows up on an invoice.

Avennex questions.

What is Avennex?

A software studio in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada, working since 2022. It builds custom software, AI integrations and workflow automation for startups and established businesses, and runs products of its own.

Is Avennex an agency?

It's a software studio. Software is the whole job, from client projects to products the studio builds and runs itself. Brand campaigns and media buying aren't part of it.

Is an offshore team or a freelancer cheaper?

Per hour, usually. Per project, it depends on how much rework, review and back and forth the hours include. Compare totals, and count your own team's time too.

Should a small business hire a developer or work with Avennex?

If the software is your product and needs daily work, hiring makes sense. For a defined project, or one that needs several skills, Avennex gets there faster without the cost of a permanent team.

Can Avennex work alongside our own developers?

Yes. It can build a piece your team doesn't have time for, or the first version before a handover.

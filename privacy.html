Privacy policy.

What we collect, why we keep it, and how to have it removed.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What we collect

When you send us a message, apply for a role or leave a comment, we keep what you give us: your name, your email and whatever you write. A job application also includes the resume you attach.

If you allow it, we also count visits with Google Analytics. More on that under Cookies below. We don't use advertising pixels.

How we use it

Messages reach our team so we can reply. Applications are read by the people hiring for that role. Comments appear publicly, next to whatever you commented on.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone for marketing.

Where it's kept

Your data sits with established cloud providers, encrypted on its way to them, and only our team can reach it. Resumes go to private storage that can't be opened from the web.

Outside services

Outside providers keep the site running: hosting, storage and email delivery. They handle data only to provide those services to us.

Academy videos play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player, which loads only when you press play. The site also remembers a small preference or two in your browser, such as whether animations are on, and your chat with Nex stays in the tab until you close it. None of it is used to track you.

Cookies

On your first visit a small banner asks before anything optional runs. Saying no changes nothing about how the site works.

  • avx_consent: remembers your cookie choice. Needed. Kept for 6 months.
  • Motion and chat settings: stored in your browser, not as cookies, so the site remembers whether animations are on and keeps your chat with Nex open in the tab. Needed. Never sent to us.
  • _ga and _ga_*: Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics. Counts visits and shows which pages get read. Kept for up to 13 months.

Google processes analytics data, mostly in the United States. We don't use it for ads and we don't share it with anyone else.

Change your mind any time with Cookie settings, also in the footer. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a no.

How long we keep it

  • Messages: until the conversation wraps up, then deleted within 90 days.
  • Job applications: 12 months after the role closes, then removed.
  • Admin accounts: as long as the account is active.

Your rights

Ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold on you. Email hello@avennex.com and it's done within 7 business days.

If you're in the EU, the UK or Quebec, you can also object to how we use your data, ask for a copy you can take elsewhere, and complain to your local privacy regulator. In Canada that's the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, and in Quebec the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Anything significant gets a note on the homepage too.

Questions

Email hello@avennex.com. You'll hear back from someone on the team. The same address reaches the person responsible for privacy at Avennex.

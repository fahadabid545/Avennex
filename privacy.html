Privacy Policy

How we handle your data. Plain language, no legal tricks.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

What we collect

When you fill out a contact form or submit a job application, we collect your name, email, and whatever you type into the message fields. That's it.

Our site doesn't use tracking cookies. We don't run third-party analytics scripts. No pixel trackers, no retargeting.

How we use it

Contact form submissions go straight to our team inbox so we can reply to you. Job applications are reviewed internally and stored for the hiring process.

We won't sell your information. We won't share it with third parties for marketing. The data exists for one reason: so we can respond to you.

Where it's stored

Data lives in Supabase (PostgreSQL), hosted on AWS infrastructure. Connections are encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage.

How long we keep it

Contact messages: until the conversation is resolved, then deleted within 90 days.

Job applications: kept for 12 months after the role closes, then removed.

Account data (admin only): kept as long as the account is active.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete any data we have on you. Send an email to hello@avennex.com and we'll handle it within 7 business days.

Third-party services

We use Google Fonts for typography (loaded from Google's CDN). Google may log font requests per their own privacy policy. No other third-party services process your personal data through our site.

Changes

If we update this policy, the "last updated" date changes above. Major changes get a note on the homepage. We won't sneak anything in.

Questions

Email hello@avennex.com with any privacy questions.

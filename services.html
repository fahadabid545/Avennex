Custom software and SaaS

Off-the-shelf tools get you most of the way. Then the last stretch costs more in workarounds than a proper build would. We write software shaped around how your business actually runs, and keep it tidy enough that the next team can pick it up.

What it covers

  • Internal tools and admin systems
  • Customer-facing platforms
  • SaaS products, from first version to paying users
  • Rebuilding legacy systems nobody wants to touch

AI integration

Workflow automation

Most businesses run on a handful of repeated steps that live between systems. We map them as they really happen, automate the parts that don't need judgement, and leave people with the parts that do.

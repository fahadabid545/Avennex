Zapier, Make or custom code?

We build automations on Zapier, on Make and in code. Here's how we pick between them.

Which automation tool to use.

Zapier and Make cover most business automations, quickly and without a developer on call. Custom code fits when a workflow runs at high volume, needs logic the builders can't express cleanly, or handles data that shouldn't pass through a third party. Plenty of setups use both.

Picking the right tool.

When Zapier or Make fits

  • The workflow has a handful of steps.
  • The apps involved already have Zapier or Make connectors.
  • The volume fits inside a plan you're happy paying for.
  • You want it running this week.
  • Your team wants to change it without a developer.

When custom code fits

  • Task volume makes the plan cost more than a build would.
  • A failed run needs custom retries, checks or rollbacks.
  • The logic outgrows paths and routers, or needs heavy data processing.
  • Sensitive data shouldn't pass through another platform.

Using both.

You don't have to pick one. Simple zaps can stay where they are while the heavy, high volume flow moves to code.

Some flows start in Make, prove their value and later move to code. Others stay in Zapier for years, and that's fine. Our workflow automation work covers both, so a move later doesn't mean switching teams.

What automation costs at volume.

Look at the bill at the volume you expect in a year, not today's. Usage based pricing is cheap for small flows and grows with every task.

Then add upkeep on both sides. Time spent fixing runs in a builder, hosting and updates for code. Code costs more on day one, and its bill usually grows more slowly.

Zapier, Make and custom code FAQ.

Is Zapier enough for a small business?

Often, yes. For short workflows at modest volume it's quick and affordable. Long flows and high volume are where the plan and the upkeep start to add up.

When should we move off Zapier or Make?

When the plan costs more than a build would, when a failed step costs real money, or when the logic no longer fits on one screen. Check first whether restructuring the flow fixes it. Often it does.

Can AI steps be part of an automation?

Yes. Zapier and Make both offer AI steps for reading documents, sorting messages and drafting replies. Code gives more control over prompts and checks. Whichever runs it, test the step on your own data before anyone relies on it.

Does Avennex build on Zapier and Make?

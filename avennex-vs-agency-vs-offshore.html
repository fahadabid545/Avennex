Avennex, an agency or an offshore team?

Three ways to get software built, compared on what buyers ask about in a first call.

The difference, in short.

Agencies suit large programs where software sits next to brand and marketing work. Offshore teams compete on hourly rate when the spec is fixed. Avennex starts from the problem, lays out more than one way to solve it and prices the plan around your budget. Its own products run in public, so the standard of work is visible before anyone signs.

When the other two fit.

  • An agency: software is one piece of a bigger push that also needs brand, media and content work.
  • An offshore team: the spec is detailed and fixed, someone on your side reviews the code, and hourly rate is the main concern.

Avennex questions.

What is Avennex?

A software studio in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada, working since 2022. It builds custom software, AI integrations and workflow automation for startups and established businesses, and runs products of its own.

Is Avennex an agency?

It's a software studio. Software is the whole job, from client projects to products the studio builds and runs itself. Brand campaigns and media buying aren't part of it.

Is an offshore team cheaper?

Per hour, usually. Per project, it depends on how much rework, review and back and forth the hours include. Compare totals, and count your own team's time too.

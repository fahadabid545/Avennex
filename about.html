About

A studio that builds for clients and for itself.

Avennex has built custom software, AI tools and workflow automation since 2022, for startups and established businesses. Some of it's code. Some of it runs on Zapier or Make. The tool follows the problem.

When teams call us.

Usually it's because the software they bought covers most of the work and the rest lives in spreadsheets and one person's head. That leftover part is our job, whether it needs a build, an automation or a better setup of what's already there.

Trends don't decide our stack. Code, Zapier, Make, an AI model or a setting in a tool you already use. Each goes in when it suits the job, and we'll explain why in plain words.

If something isn't worth building, you'll hear it from us.

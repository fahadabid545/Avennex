About

We build for your goals, not our template.

Avennex builds custom software, AI tools and automation for startups and established businesses. We've been at it since 2022.

Where we come in.

Most teams find us after off-the-shelf software stopped fitting. The tool handles most of the job, and the rest lives in spreadsheets, workarounds and one person's memory. That last stretch is where we work.

We don't chase trends. We use modern tools because they hold up: cloud infrastructure, clean APIs, and machine learning where it earns its place. The stack follows from what you're trying to achieve, never the other way round.

If something isn't worth building, we'll say so. Sometimes the fix is a setting in a tool you already pay for.

You get a team that treats your success as its own. Honestly, it is.
